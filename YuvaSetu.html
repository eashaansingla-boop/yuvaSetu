For children growing up with less

Every child deserves a bridge to somewhere they belong.

YuvaSetu runs after-school centres where children from low-income neighbourhoods get help with schoolwork, a trusted adult to talk to, and friends who show up every day.

Support a child See what we do

setu means bridge, and that is the whole idea.

Why we exist

Poverty takes more than money from a child.

It takes attention. Children in crowded homes and stretched families often go a whole day without an adult asking how they are.

It takes confidence. A child who has fallen behind in class starts to believe they are the problem. They are not.

It takes a place to belong. We make one: a room, a routine, and people who remember your name and your favourite game.

Programmes

Four ways we walk alongside a child

Learning comes first, but a child learns better when they feel safe, heard and part of something. Each programme covers one piece of that.

Padhai GharAfter-school learning

Ages 6 to 14
Mon to Sat

Small groups with trained volunteers for homework, reading and maths. Nobody is rushed, and every child gets a snack before they start.

SunnoCircles for feelings

Ages 8 to 16
Weekly

A counsellor-led circle where children talk, draw and play games about worries, anger, friendship and home. What is said in the circle stays in the circle.

HunarSkills and arts for teens

Ages 12 to 18
Weekends

Theatre, digital skills, stitching and sport, taught by local mentors. Teens build something they can show people and feel proud of.

ParivaarSupport for families

Whole family
Monthly

Home visits, help with school admissions and documents, and parent meetings, so the support a child gets at the centre is backed up at home.

A day with us

Three hours that change the rest of the evening

Routine is the point. Children know what comes next, who will be there, and that they are expected.

come as you are, hungry or tired or shy.

  1. 3:30

    Arrive and eat

    A hot snack and a check-in at the door. Everyone is greeted by name.

  2. 4:00

    Homework and reading

    Small groups by grade, with a volunteer who stays with the same children all term.

  3. 5:15

    Sunno circle

    Twenty minutes to talk, draw or just sit with others. Counsellors lead; no one has to speak.

  4. 5:45

    Play, art or sport

    Open time to run around, make things and be a child.

  5. 6:30

    Walk home together

    Children head home in groups, with a staff member at each stage of the route.
